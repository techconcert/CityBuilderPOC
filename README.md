# CityBuilderPOC

A small, in-browser proof of concept for a frozen-city colony builder, inspired by *Frostpunk* and *Frozen City*, rendered in an isometric 2.5D style with AI-generated graphics.

## Purpose

This repository exists to test whether the core loop of a cold-climate colony builder can be made to feel good in a lightweight browser game. It is an experiment, not a product: the goal is to learn what works before investing in a full game.

## Concept

You guide a small settlement of survivors in a frozen world: gather resources, place buildings, and keep the colony growing.

The POC deliberately keeps scope small. It is **not** a full *Frostpunk* clone, and it intentionally leaves out:

- the central upgrade mechanic (the big generator/furnace at the heart of the city),
- the hot/cold heat simulation,
- heroes.

In feel, it sits closer to light idle colony games such as *Survivor Island Idle* than to a deep survival strategy game.

## Art direction

- **Isometric 2.5D**: a fixed-angle isometric view of the settlement and its surroundings.
- **AI-generated graphics**: tiles, buildings and props are produced with AI image generation and then assembled into the isometric scene.
- **Frozen setting**: snow, ice and a cold palette set the tone of the world.

## Status

🧪 **Proof of concept.** Early and experimental. Expect missing features, placeholder art and breaking changes. Nothing here is final.
